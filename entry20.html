HOMEBASE LOG 004

Cam: …

Commish: Never thought I would feel emotional about a squirrel.

Commish: In any regard, thank you so much Clara. Your help has been phenomenal in dealing with this.

Clara: Of course! When Sidney told me about this project, I wasn't expecting it to be so incredible! Your trust in this squirrel goes beyond anything I've seen in my days in operations. I've seen idiots. I've seen AI robot dogs. Never seen a rodent lead the world's most important expedition!

Commish: Well, remember: you're a part of this project now. With the clearance comes a lot of responsibility. No telling about what went down today, got it?

Clara: I would never, Commissioner! I'm here for the job first and foremost! I give you my German seal of approval.

Commish: Whatever. Good day today, everyone. It has been a chaotic day and with Cosmo's cryo-sleep, we can now rest. Get a coffee. A pastry. Pat yourself on the back.

Sidney: And tomorrow?

Commish: Tomorrow, we go back to work. Now we need to monitor Cosmo more thoroughly. If our timecards are right, Cosmo will be in open space in six months. He'll be as far as any human has been. Asteroids will be more plentiful and fuel will become more sparse.

Cam: So more up-time for Cosmo?

Commish: Correct. And I don't want to see him leaving the command-room. Ever.

Clara: He will age, no? Zis seems like we will cut it close with his aging factor, if he ages like a normal squirrel.

Commish: This will be tomorrow's affair. I will get in contact with some scientists and they'll let us know what we can do from Homebase. Go home for now, see your pets. Water your plants.

Sidney: I might stop by the brasserie after. Cam, you in?

Clara: Ooh! I am in, Sid! I want to dance zis night away drunk off French liquor!

Sidney: Nevermind, I'm feeling quite sick.

Commish: Cameron.

Cam: You're talking to me like you're my mom.

Commish: Today does not change anything. We can't get too attached to an animal that serves its duty like a pet. Do you know the main duty of a soldier on-duty?

Cam: What? Shoot accurately?

Commish: Follow your duty. To a tee. Listen to your head-command. If we act disorganized, we will quickly fall apart and this operation will be a fiery failure - along with our careers. Every time, we must act as a unified force. If feelings get in the way, we have to recalibrate. Do you know what 'recalibrate' means in this context?

Cam: …

Commish: It means you'll be moved to another department. So ship up, or ship out. No shits given for your rank or how well I know you personally. The head council wants progress. Understood?

Cam: Got it.
